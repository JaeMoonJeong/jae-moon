Sustainable Energy System Optimization

Advancing high-efficiency energy storage system through structural synergy and advanced materials.

Situation

  • Global energy paradigm shift toward carbon neutrality and hydrogen-based systems.
  • Strategic necessity for core technical synergy between PEMFC and VRFB infrastructures.

Task

  • Optimization of energy efficiency via interfacial resistance minimization.
  • Development of high-performance components including Bipolar Plates and advanced Electrodes.
  • Enhancement of electrolyte permeability and mass transfer in flow battery systems.

Action

  • Full-cycle system engineering: From individual part fabrication to full cell assembly and testing.
  • Study 1 (Electrode Morphology):
    Fabrication of holey-patterned carbon paper electrodes to induce pseudo-channel effects for uniform electrolyte distribution.
  • Study 2 (Interfacial Integration):
    Design of contact-resistance-free integrated BP-electrode structures via graphite fiber interconnection and glucose impregnation.
  • Study 3 (Stiffness Gradient):
    Development of stiffness-gradient electrodes to balance electrical contact points and electrolyte diffusion across the through-thickness direction.
  • Study 4 (Advanced Nanocomposite):
    Synthesis of horizontally aligned CNT nanocomposite bipolar plates (HACN-BP) for extreme electrical conductivity and zero permeability.

Result

  • Efficiency Breakthrough: Achieved up to 86.5% energy efficiency (10% increase over neat CF).
  • Ohmic Loss Mitigation: Successfully reduced potential drop in Bipolar Plates by 40%.
  • System-Level Validation: Directly performed end-to-end testing from component level to full-cell stack performance.
