Development of Composite Dimensionally Stable Anode (DSA) for Electrodeposited Copper Foil

Situation

  • Requirement for non-consumable electrodes (DSA) to maintain precise dimensions and high purity in copper foil production.
    • Excessive maintenance costs due to heavy reliance on high-priced noble metals (Iridium).
    • Production downtime and efficiency loss from frequent electrode replacement cycles.
    • Electrode corrosion and electrical conductivity imbalance in highly acidic electrolytes.

    Task

    • Next-Generation Architecture: Engineering of high-durability, cost-effective composite anodes.
      • Radical extension of replacement cycles through enhanced material stability.
      • Minimization of noble metal loading and reduction of unit costs via composite integration.
      • Stable Oxygen Evolution Reaction (OER) and minimal interfacial resistance under high current density.

      Action

      • Architecture Design of carbon-polymer composite DSA & multi-layer structure optimization

    Result

    • Lifespan & Cost: Extended replacement cycles and reduced material costs.
