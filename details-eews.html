Sustainable Energy System Optimization

장주기 다목적 무인 잠수모함 핵심기술개발

Situation

  • Demand for long-endurance unmanned systems for autonomous surveillance and reconnaissance missions.
  • Critical requirement for lightweight structural design and integrated energy storage for extended operations.

Task

  • Design of high-efficiency autonomous flight robot structures based on structural batteries.
  • Optimization of airframe integrated with advanced composite materials and energy cells.
  • Application of AI-driven system optimization for mission endurance.

Action

  • Implementation of multifuctional structure design integrating high-strength composites and battery elements.
  • Structural analysis and optimization using ANSYS Composite PrepPost (ACP) and CATIA V5.
  • Development of AI-based system path and power management optimization algorithms using Python.

Result

  • Successful maximization of energy density for significantly extended operational radius and time.
  • Innovative weight reduction through structural-battery integration.

Project Information

  • Category: Aerospace / AI & Optimization
  • Client: Agency for Defense Development (ADD)
  • Project Date: 2019.08 - 2024.07
  • Software Stack: ANSYS ACP, CATIA V5, Python
Achievements

• [Patent] Method for manufacturing structural battery composite for underwater vehicles (2023)
• [Paper] Optimization of multifunctional composite structures for long-endurance UAVs (JCM, 2024)
